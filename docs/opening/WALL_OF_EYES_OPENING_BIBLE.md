# WALL OF EYES — EXHIBITION OPENING BIBLE

Status: **LOCKED FOR IMPLEMENTATION**
Date: 2026-09-23

Visual spark:
a user-supplied reference image showing close-cropped eyes inside segmented television / broadcast frames, interrupted by saturated red fields.

The reference is mood / composition evidence only.
No frame from the reference is used as an asset.

---

## 1. ROLE

WALL OF EYES belongs to the exhibition itself.

It is **not** a source project room.

It is the first authored threshold before FIRST CONTACT.

Its job is to invert the normal portfolio relationship:

the visitor is not only looking at work;
the exhibition appears to notice the visitor first.

This should feel:
- cinematic;
- archival;
- broadcast-like;
- slightly uncanny;
- observant;
- controlled.

It must **not** feel:
- horror;
- surveillance software;
- cyberpunk;
- TikTok edit imitation;
- generic glitch aesthetic.

---

## 2. FIRST FRAME

Black field.

A wall of irregular CRT / monitor frames occupies most of the viewport.

Some frames contain original procedural eyes.
Some contain:
- red signal fields;
- calibration bars;
- dark silhouettes;
- empty broadcast noise.

The eyes are not synchronized.

A few blink.
A few drift.
One or two eventually settle toward the visitor.

The page identity remains spare:

`OMAR KHAIR`
`SELECTED WORKS / 2026`
`ENTER WITH SOUND`
`ENTER IN SILENCE`
`INDEX`

No explanation paragraph.

---

## 3. BEHAVIOUR

There is no forced timer.

The wall can remain alive indefinitely until the visitor chooses to enter.

Desktop:
- eye pupils respond to pointer position with different lag / amplitude;
- only some eyes track strongly;
- most movement is micro-movement;
- blinks use different cadences.

Mobile:
- touch movement may influence the field;
- otherwise eyes use restrained autonomous saccades;
- no motion-sensor permission.

Reduced motion:
- no continuous tracking;
- no repeated blinking;
- one strong static wall remains.

---

## 4. ENTRY EVENT

When the visitor chooses sound or silence:

1. UI copy recedes;
2. eyes stop wandering;
3. several eyes align toward centre;
4. television fields lose signal;
5. frames collapse into darkness;
6. two residual points / one relation remain;
7. route enters FIRST CONTACT.

This is the conceptual handoff:
**being watched → two unresolved presences → contact.**

No loading bar.
No unskippable 8-second film.

The event should take roughly 0.7–1.2 seconds in normal motion and almost immediately in reduced motion.

---

## 5. MATERIAL

V1 uses original procedural browser graphics.

No copyrighted film / television eye footage.

Materials:
- warm skin / amber;
- deep black;
- signal red;
- burnt orange;
- white phosphor;
- CRT scanlines;
- faint bloom;
- irregular frame borders.

Eyes must vary enough to avoid looking like repeated UI icons.

---

## 6. TYPOGRAPHY

The wall is visually loud.
The shell typography stays disciplined.

Identity should behave like a broadcast caption or gallery registration mark, not like a poster pasted over the eyes.

---

## 7. SOUND

No autoplay.

Before entry: silence.

If ENTER WITH SOUND is chosen:
- the shared AudioContext is unlocked;
- a tiny wake / phosphor click may occur only if authored later;
- FIRST CONTACT owns the first meaningful sonic material.

If ENTER IN SILENCE is chosen:
- exact same visual opening;
- no nag.

---

## 8. REUSE LATER

The eye wall is an exhibition-owned motif, not a wallpaper.

V1 recurrence allowance:
- opening: full strength;
- at most one brief transition echo later;
- AFTERIMAGE may reuse a single witness frame if it earns meaning.

Do not put blinking eyes behind every room.

---

## 9. IMPLEMENTATION STANDARD

Close only when:
- desktop wall reads as irregular television architecture;
- mobile crop still feels intentional;
- at least several eyes have distinct anatomy / timing;
- pointer response is subtle;
- blinks are non-synchronized;
- red fields punctuate rather than dominate;
- no external footage is required;
- sound / silence entry both work;
- INDEX remains reachable;
- reduced motion has a designed still edition;
- entry hands off cleanly to FIRST CONTACT;
- lint / typecheck / build are green;
- browser captures are reviewed.
