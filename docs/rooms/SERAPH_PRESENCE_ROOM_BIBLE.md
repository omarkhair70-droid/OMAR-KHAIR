## 6. PERFORMANCE GRAMMAR

The exhibition cut uses the active source grammar:

```
DORMANT
  ↓
NOTICE
  ↙      ↘
ATTUNE   STRAIN
  ↓        ↓
RELEASE  FRACTURE
             ↓
         AFTERMATH
             ↓
           REFORM
```

### DORMANT

SERARA is present before reaction.

Stillness is not deadness.

No dramatic timer event.

### NOTICE

Presence is sensed.

Gaze / head / chamber direction begin to acknowledge the visitor after delay.

No reward animation.

### ATTUNE

Stillness + recognition accumulate.

Expected:
- incidental movement reduces;
- gaze settles;
- posture composes;
- sound becomes clearer / warmer;
- internal signal becomes more coherent.

A calm visitor may remain here and **never cause fracture**.

### STRAIN

Repeated disturbance / fast movement / touch pressure accumulate.

Expected:
- asymmetry increases;
- posture tightens;
- internal heat rises;
- sound becomes unstable;
- chamber and body agree.

### FRACTURE

Earned threshold.

A single click can never equal rupture.

Fracture occurs only after accumulated relationship pressure.

### AFTERMATH

Consequence remains.

No snap back to neutral.

This is the visual peak.

### REFORM

Same being returns progressively.

Residue survives.

## 7. EXHIBITION-SPECIFIC EXIT

SERAPH must not force every visitor through fracture.

Two valid exits exist.

### CALM EXIT

After meaningful ATTUNE:
- SERARA settles;
- one mineral seam appears near the floor / body;
- visitor can follow it toward FOKHARA.

The room does not punish calmness with less “content”.

### CONSEQUENCE EXIT

After FRACTURE → AFTERMATH → REFORM:
- heat decays;
- one topology / material seam remains;
- the same seam becomes mineral as FOKHARA arrives.

Both exits produce:
`seraph-residue = seam`

The residue can carry a variant:
- `attuned-seam`
- `reformed-seam`

AFTERIMAGE may later reflect which state was experienced.
