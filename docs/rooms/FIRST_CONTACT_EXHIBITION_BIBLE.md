## 2. TARGETED EXTERNAL RESEARCH

This is a narrow interaction-law check, not a visual-reference moodboard.

### Rafael Lozano-Hemmer — Pulse Room

Useful principle:
one simple bodily gesture produces a perceivable state and then becomes part of the work's memory.

Borrow:
- input becomes residue;
- the system records a trace beyond the immediate gesture.

Do not borrow:
- biometric sensing;
- bulb grid;
- visual form.

Official source:
https://www.lozano-hemmer.com/pulse_room.php

### Hello Lamp Post — PAN Studio / Playable City

Useful principle:
a familiar physical object can be "woken" through one legible action, with very little instruction.

Borrow:
- the object is the threshold;
- one clear action is enough;
- technology should disappear behind the encounter.

Do not borrow:
- conversational UI;
- SMS language;
- anthropomorphic city-object personality.

Official project source:
https://www.playablecity.com/projects/hello-lamp-post/

### RANDOM INTERNATIONAL — Rain Room

Useful principle:
human presence changes the environment without giving the visitor conventional direct controls.

Borrow:
- presence causes environmental consequence;
- the visitor learns the relation by experiencing it.

Do not borrow:
- tracking-camera model;
- spectacle scale;
- rain metaphor.

Official source:
https://www.random-international.com/rain-room

### Synthesis

The room should therefore use:

**one clear wake gesture**
→ **one relation that changes**
→ **one trace that persists**
→ **no dashboard / tutorial / score**

The visitor should feel that the world responded, not that they operated a 3D interface.

## 3. ROOM ARC

The exhibition cut has five states:

```
OBJECT
  ↓
GAP
  ↓
ANGLE
  ↓
SIGNAL
  ↓
THIRD THING
  ↓
RESIDUE / SERAPH
```

### OBJECT

First frame:
a softly asymmetric porcelain / mineral field object.

Copy:
`OBJECT 001`
`TAP / ONLY IF CURIOUS`

Gesture:
tap / click / Enter.

Consequence:
sound wakes if globally enabled;
one small haptic tick on supported mobile;
the object opens into relation.

### GAP

Two masses.
Close in space, unresolved in meaning.

Copy:
`SMALL DISTANCE / UNKNOWN MEANING`

Gesture:
continue by touching the field.

Consequence:
the camera / relation shifts toward ANGLE.

### ANGLE

Two fragments initially read as misaligned.

Copy:
`THE THING DIDN'T CHANGE. THE ANGLE DID.`

Gesture:
press / hold or deliberate second touch.

Consequence:
the fragments align enough to become calm, but not perfect.

### SIGNAL

Two nodes remain separate.

Copy:
`A SIGNAL CAN EXIST WITHOUT ARRIVING.`

Gesture:
hold.

Consequence:
early pulses stall / fade;
sustained intent allows one signal to cross.

No skill-game timing window.

### THIRD THING

Neither node closes the distance.

A new membrane / form appears in the gap.

No explanatory headline required after arrival.

Gesture:
touching the shared form wakes local warmth.

Consequence:
the room records one residue:
`warm-seam`.

The shared form never resolves into 100% completion.

## 4. TRANSITION TO SERAPH

The final shared form does not become a portal.

It collapses until only one incomplete warm seam remains.

The field darkens.

The seam changes from relational bridge to bodily contour.

SERAPH owns what happens after the seam becomes presence.

## 5. SOUND

Use the exhibition's shared AudioContext.

No new AudioContext per room.

When sound is globally ON:
- OBJECT: ceramic wake + low air;
- GAP: dry room tone;
- ANGLE: paired ceramic ticks;
- SIGNAL: sparse glass / sine pulses;
- THIRD THING: first stable harmonic warmth;
- RESIDUE: reduce toward one unresolved tone.

When sound is OFF:
nothing nags the visitor.

The room remains complete.

## 6. HAPTICS

Supported mobile only.

- wake: one short tick;
- angle alignment: two soft pulses;
- first successful signal: one travelling-like pulse;
- third thing: one soft longer pulse.

No vibration on every tap.

## 8. DESKTOP

Inputs:
- click / hold;
- pointer changes view by very small amounts only.

Pointer must not rotate the scene like a model viewer.

Camera response is damped and low-amplitude.
